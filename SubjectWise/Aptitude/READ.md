## 🎯 Preparation Strategy

**Learn → Practice → PYQs → Revise → Mock Tests**

1. Understand the basic concepts.
2. Practice topic-wise questions.
3. Solve **GATE Previous Year Questions (PYQs)**.
4. Maintain a formula and shortcut notebook.
5. Analyze your mistakes.
6. Take timed mock tests to improve speed and accuracy.

---

## ⭐ Important Tip

Don't just watch lectures. **Practice is more important than watching videos.**

For every topic:

> **Concept → Examples → Practice Questions → PYQs → Revision**
